A picture is never drawn larger than its own size. Every picture in this post is small, so on a wide screen each one should sit in the middle of the page at the size printed on it, sharp, with empty space on both sides.

The header above is 480 pixels wide. It shrinks to fit a phone screen, and that is the only time it should change size.
