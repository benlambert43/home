A picture is limited in two ways: it is never drawn larger than its own size, and it is never taller than a set height. That height is lower on smaller screens than on wide ones, and every picture in this post is sized to fall between the two.

The header above is 720 by 480. On a smaller screen it is over the height limit, so it is scaled down. On a wide screen it is under the limit, so it should be shown at its own size and no larger.
